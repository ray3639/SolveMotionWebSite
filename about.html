Where the Water Comes From

Set on the Island of North Haven, Maine, Where the Water Comes From is a literary novel about continuity, restraint, and belonging.

The story moves quietly through childhood, work, departure, aging, and return - through boat crossings, shipyard mornings, family walks, and the unseen rules that govern island life.

"What lasts is not what is taken, but what is tended."

The Shape of the Shore

The Shape of the Shore is shaped by real water: a pond that behaves like a source, rivers that teach movement, shores that alter what reaches them, and ocean weather that does not ask permission before changing a map.

It is also shaped by family papers, kept objects, work, marriage, parenthood, fear, return, and the difficult mercy of deciding what should travel forward.

"What lasts is not what is untouched, but what is tended after the weather has passed."
