Capabilities Governance

Capability language is governed so the page separates active platform capability, Core-owned intelligence capability, and commercial direction without overstating implementation state or creating duplicate app-side logic.

Active Capability Boundary

Active capabilities are the Core 12.0 outputs, four completed Core-connected 6.0 application surfaces, reports, confidence, risk, recommendations, missing evidence, trajectory, trend, and governed DTO rendering already represented across the current product system.

Core-Owned Capability Boundary

Capability depth belongs in Core 12.0 where scoring, readiness, risk, confidence, recommendations, missing evidence, report narratives, and domain interpretation can remain deterministic, explainable, and reusable.

Commercial Direction Boundary

Enterprise deployment, licensing, white-label surfaces, SaaS, SDK/API, partner review, and investor review are governed commercial directions. They should remain tied to release gates, baselines, and production verification.

Capability Truth Boundary

Capabilities should be described as decision-support and education-support infrastructure. The page should not imply clinical authority, legal authority, emergency use, pharmacy policy replacement, or completion of every commercial channel.
