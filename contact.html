How to take part

  1. Request an invitation. Use the app's email link with the Google Account email you use in Google Play, your Android device and country. Send only if you want the release owner to contact you about the selected test. Do not send passwords or confidential records.
  2. Join the confirmed closed test. The release owner checks access and provides the app's Google Play opt-in steps. Sign in with the invited account, opt in and install through Google Play. An email request or a direct APK download does not enroll you.
  3. Test and share useful feedback. Stay opted in for at least 14 consecutive days after joining. Try the available features and report the app version, Android version, steps and what happened through the existing email channel or Google Play private feedback.

Already invited to SolvePharmacy? Once your account has been added, open the SolvePharmacy closed test on Google Play. Access depends on your account's eligibility for that track.

Invitations and release availability are confirmed per app. This website does not collect a tester list or enroll you automatically. Opening an email link does not send a message. Read Google's closed-testing requirements and our privacy guidance.
