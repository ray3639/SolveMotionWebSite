Contact

SolveMotion Labs is built to be useful and transparent. If you have questions, feedback, or run into issues, reaching out is always welcome.

How to reach us

Email is the primary point of contact:

Email: ray3639@comcast.net

This inbox is used for product-related communication, feedback, and issue reports.

When it makes sense to reach out

  • Questions about how a specific application works
  • Bug reports or download issues
  • Suggestions that improve clarity or usability
  • Feedback on guidance, explanations, or structure
  • Investor, enterprise, licensing, white-label, SDK/API, or Core 12.0 partnership inquiries

What this lab can and can't help with

SolveMotion Labs provides tools and guidance, but it does not replace professional services.

  • We can help with: product questions, feature intent, and usability feedback
  • We cannot provide: medical advice, legal opinions, or guaranteed outcomes
  • We do not offer: consulting engagements or data resale

Response expectations

Messages are read personally and thoughtfully. Response times may vary, but every legitimate inquiry is taken seriously.

Clear, concise messages help ensure the best response.

Privacy and respect

Messages are used only to respond to the inquiry at hand. There is no mailing list, no automated marketing, and no data sharing with third parties.

Respectful communication is expected and reciprocated.

Governed contact pathways

Contact requests are triaged by purpose so product support, enterprise licensing, partner intake, research collaboration, Core 12.0 external-app inquiries, and general feedback can stay aligned with the correct review path.

  • Product support: app questions, download issues, clarity concerns, usability feedback, and release-center questions.
  • Enterprise and licensing: white-label deployment, commercial readiness, Core 12.0 as a Service, and organization-level platform discussions.
  • Partner intake: company-owned application workflows, approved DomainSignal input, immutable Core DTO output, and render-only app integration expectations.
  • Research collaboration: education, applied intelligence, methodology, doctrine, evidence review, and domain knowledge expansion discussions.

Clear subject lines help route the message. For example: Product Support, Enterprise Licensing, Partner Intake, Research Collaboration, or Core 12.0 App Integration.
