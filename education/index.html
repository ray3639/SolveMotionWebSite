Education Governance

Education content is governed so SolveMotion Labs can describe structured learning, applied intelligence domains, and Core-supported knowledge pathways without claiming accreditation, certification, licensure, or replacement for professional instruction.

Educational Use Boundary

Education pages support learning orientation, domain literacy, and applied reasoning structure. They should not be presented as clinical, legal, pharmacy, academic, or professional credential programs unless a verified program authority exists.

Core 12 Learning Support Boundary

SolveMotion Labs Core 12.0 supports education by centralizing DomainSignal input, deterministic reasoning, DTO output, confidence, risk, missing evidence, recommendations, and report-ready explanations across approved domains.

Credential Boundary

Learning material may explain concepts, workflows, and reasoning patterns. It should not imply certificates, accreditation, professional licensing, school credit, or regulatory approval unless those items are formally created and verified.

Professional Instruction Boundary

Educational surfaces should complement human review and domain learning. They should not replace teachers, clinicians, pharmacists, investigators, legal professionals, workplace policy, or regulated professional judgment.
