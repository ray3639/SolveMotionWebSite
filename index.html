Three responsibilities. Three separate owners.

Deterministic intelligence, governed reasoning, and controlled execution are connected parts of the workflow, but they are not the same responsibility and they do not share one runtime owner.

Deterministic intelligence

Core 12

Applies shared deterministic intelligence through established rules and repeatable logic.

Does not own: Governed reasoning, controlled execution, lifecycle truth, or consequential authorization.

Governed reasoning

RayAI

Applies governed reasoning and context where that ownership is established.

Does not own: Shared deterministic intelligence, controlled execution, lifecycle truth, or consequential authorization.

Controlled execution

Command Center

Owns controlled execution, build, release, and operational command where established.

Does not own: Governed reasoning, shared deterministic intelligence, lifecycle truth, or consequential authorization.

Core 12 deterministic intelligence can provide established inputs without becoming the reasoning owner.

RayAI governed reasoning can use established context and intelligence without becoming the execution owner.

Command Center can receive an authorized handoff for controlled execution without becoming the reasoning or deterministic-intelligence owner.

The website explains these relationships only; it does not perform reasoning, deterministic intelligence, or controlled execution.

Authority boundaries

Each owner has a defined responsibility. These boundaries prevent reasoning, deterministic intelligence, lifecycle truth, authorization, and execution from collapsing into duplicate ownership.

Core 12
Owns: Shared deterministic intelligence. Boundary: Does not become the governed reasoning, authentication, authorization, lifecycle-truth, or controlled-execution owner.
RayAI
Owns: Governed reasoning and context where established. Boundary: Does not replace Core 12 deterministic intelligence, Project Manifest lifecycle truth, or Command Center controlled execution.
Studio
Owns: Governed creation and orchestration. Boundary: Coordinates the governed workflow without becoming a second reasoning, deterministic-intelligence, authorization, lifecycle, or execution engine.
Command Center
Owns: Controlled execution, build, release, and operational command where established. Boundary: Does not become the reasoning, deterministic-intelligence, or lifecycle-truth owner.
Project Manifest
Owns: Authoritative lifecycle truth where established. Boundary: Provides lifecycle truth and context without becoming another runtime reasoning or execution agent.
Human authorization
Owns: Consequential approval and decision authority. Boundary: AI, automation, orchestration, and deterministic intelligence may inform the decision, but final consequential authority remains human.

The website explains these boundaries without implementing or duplicating their runtime ownership.

Human authorization points

AI can accelerate the work. People authorize the consequential action.

SolveMotion Labs keeps explicit human approval at the points where governed analysis becomes consequential execution, access, deployment, release, or lifecycle change.

01 Scope or lifecycle change

A person approves consequential changes to project scope, requirements, lifecycle state, or authoritative project direction.

02 Controlled execution or build

Execution, build, or other controlled operational actions proceed only through established authorization and execution owners.

03 Deployment or release

Deployment, production publication, and release decisions require explicit human authorization before consequential action occurs.

04 Protected or credentialed access

Actions involving credentials, protected systems, accounts, or restricted resources remain approval-gated through their established owners.

05 Final lifecycle decision

AI, deterministic intelligence, and orchestration can inform the decision, but final consequential lifecycle authority remains human.

This public explanation does not implement authentication or authorization services. RayAI, Studio, Command Center, and other established runtime owners retain their existing responsibilities.
