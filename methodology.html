From question to reviewed result.

01

Inspect and clarify

Understand the question, source context, existing capability and limits before proposing a change.

02

Use the established owner

Keep shared intelligence, project planning, private access and execution with their responsible components.

03

Implement and verify

Make a bounded change and check its behavior, evidence, accessibility and affected integrations.

04

Review and authorize

Present results and open gaps. People decide whether consequential next steps are appropriate.

Evidence has boundaries.

Core 12 provides shared deterministic intelligence where its contract applies. Product behavior, scientific validity, release readiness and access acceptance require their own checks. A successful software test does not prove a clinical or scientific conclusion.

Software discovery and a proposed Blueprint do not authorize development, payment, private access or release. Each transition retains the established owner and human review.
