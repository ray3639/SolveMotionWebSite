Metrics Governance

Metrics are governed so the page can describe measurable interest signals without inventing traffic, adoption, financial, investor, or customer totals. Metrics should support truthful traction review, not replace verified analytics data.

Metric Ownership Boundary

Website events, launch clicks, download clicks, Core 12.0 service interest, enterprise review interest, report interest, and contact intent are interest signals. They are governed access totals until a privacy-safe analytics provider records and verifies them.

Interpretation Boundary

Confidence, risk, readiness, recommendation, evidence, trend, trajectory, and report metrics should be interpreted through Core-owned outputs and verified website events, not through manually invented counters or unsupported claims.

No Recompute Boundary

Product and website pages may display approved Core DTO output and verified analytics summaries. They should not recompute Core-owned intelligence, infer adoption, or present unverified engagement totals as production facts.

Metrics Truth Boundary

Public metrics should remain privacy-safe, source-backed, and clearly labeled. Counts should be published only after real data exists, the measurement source is verified, and the result does not expose personal, member, form, or sensitive information.
