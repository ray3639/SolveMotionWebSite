Commercialization - Partner Review - Investor Alignment - Acquisition Readiness

Partner Readiness Path

Partnership review should move through a controlled evaluation path before any licensing, white-label, SDK/API, or Core 12.0 as a Service discussion becomes implementation work. This keeps commercial conversations aligned with governance, release gates, and truthful capability boundaries.

1. Fit Review

Confirm the domain, user group, workflow, and business process that a partner application would support.

2. Core Contract Review

Map the partner need to DomainSignal input, CoreIntelligenceOrchestrator processing, and immutable DTO output.

3. Governance Review

Confirm release gate expectations, no-local-computation boundaries, privacy-safe measurement, and human-governed use.

4. Commercial Path

Classify the opportunity as licensing, white-label deployment, SDK/API planning, Core 12.0 as a Service, or research collaboration.
