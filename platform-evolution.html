Governance and Release Discipline

The platform evolution is protected by clear development discipline: inspect first, avoid blind edits, prevent duplicate logic, preserve ASCII / NO_BOM, create central baselines, and keep doctrine updated as the website changes.

  • Inspect actual files before patching.
  • Use deterministic PowerShell 5.1 workflows.
  • Use central baselines under local development pathsrc\_BASELINES.
  • Use PRE_PATCH backups only under local development pathsrc\_BASELINES\PRE_PATCH.
  • Keep pages ASCII / NO_BOM clean.
  • Verify tag balance, footer count, required terms, and download artifacts.
  • Do not publish inspection reports unless intentionally needed.

Platform Evolution Governance

Platform evolution must stay release-gated, doctrine-aligned, and commercially grounded. New roadmap language should explain direction without turning future capability into an unsupported production claim.

  • Roadmap discipline: future website layers should move through inspect, patch, verify, production check, doctrine closeout, and baseline gates.
  • Future capability boundary: capability matrices, investor pages, enterprise pages, research expansion, and service packaging should remain planned direction until implemented and verified.
  • Commercial evolution boundary: licensing, white-label deployment, SDK/API direction, and Core 12.0 as a Service should be described as governed commercialization paths.
  • Core 12.0 service alignment: platform growth should preserve DomainSignal input, CoreIntelligenceOrchestrator execution, immutable DTO output, and render-only application surfaces.
  • Release-gated growth: each new public claim should stay tied to pushed commits, production verification, artifact status, and doctrine updates.
  • No-overstatement rule: platform evolution must not imply live public SaaS availability, unrestricted API access, unsupported customer adoption, or completed-product status for in-progress work.

This keeps the evolution story strong enough for investor, enterprise, partner, and acquisition review while preserving traceable proof behind each public website claim.
