What It Does

SolveCrime 6.0 organizes investigation context into structured intelligence surfaces. The app supports case reasoning, evidence review, scene intelligence, timeline interpretation, report summaries, and next action recommendations while preserving human-governed review.

Case Reasoning

Helps organize case facts, questions, working interpretations, open issues, and decision points into reviewable investigation context.

Evidence Review

Supports evidence organization, missing evidence visibility, relationship review, and caution around unsupported conclusions.

Scene Intelligence

Frames scene observations, reconstruction direction, diagram concepts, and timeline logic as structured review surfaces.

Reports

Converts case context into report-ready investigation summaries, governed recommendations, and next action review.

Boundaries

SolveCrime is not a law enforcement authority, legal decision system, forensic certification system, or substitute for professional investigation. It does not make legal conclusions or determine guilt. Its role is to organize investigation context, explain uncertainty, surface missing evidence, and support human-governed review.
