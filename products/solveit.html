SolveIt

Video-based incident understanding and scenario analysis.

NEW • SolveIt 2.0 Capabilities

SolveIt 2.0 introduces an on-device AI reasoning engine designed to support clear, structured incident understanding without forcing conclusions. The system prioritizes transparency, learning, and evidence preservation.

  • On-device AI engine for scenario reasoning
  • Structured video-based environment capture
  • Preservation of spatial and contextual evidence
  • Exploration of multiple plausible interpretations
  • No black-box decisions or hidden assumptions
  • Clear explanation surfaces for review and learning
  • Designed for education, training, and accountability

Who it’s for

  • Incident analysis and review
  • Training and educational environments
  • Scenario-based learning
  • Users who value clarity over certainty

How it’s used

Capture an environment, explore possible interpretations, and review outcomes using structured reasoning tools that emphasize transparency and context.

Platform & Intelligence

SolveIt 2.0 is built on the SolveMotion Labs intelligence platform, sharing core architectural principles with SolveMotion 2.0. The system is designed to evolve safely with AI while remaining explainable and human-centered.
