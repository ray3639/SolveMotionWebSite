Boundaries

SolveMind is not a medical, psychiatric, or diagnostic system. It does not replace clinical care, counseling, professional judgment, or emergency support. Cognitive readiness, stress context, confidence, risk, missing evidence, and recommendations should be treated as decision-support context for human-governed review.
