SolveMotion Core Capabilities (Established in 2.0)

  • Real-time movement capture using the device camera
  • On-device pose detection and skeletal modeling
  • Frame-by-frame analysis with visual playback and thumbnails
  • Automatic repetition detection and movement segmentation
  • Stability, control, and consistency scoring
  • Risk, readiness, and quality metrics per session
  • Historical session storage and comparison
  • AI-generated summaries and movement-specific recommendations
  • Fully offline-capable core analysis (no account required)

AI & Analysis Engine

SolveMotion 2.0 includes a dedicated on-device AI analysis engine that evaluates movement patterns across time, not just individual frames.

  • Multi-metric session analytics (quality, readiness, risk)
  • Trend-aware scoring across multiple sessions
  • Movement-specific analysis logic
  • Designed for real-world variability

Health & Safety Metrics

Health-related signals are informed by guidance from the CDC and WHO, combined with biomechanical best practices.

  • Fatigue and stability indicators
  • Risk awareness signals (non-diagnostic)
  • Readiness guidance aligned with public health standards

Privacy & Design Philosophy

  • No accounts or required cloud services
  • On-device analysis by default
  • Privacy-first by design

Who SolveMotion Is For

  • Kinesiology students and educators

How It’s Used

Record sessions, review insights, track progress over time, and make informed training and recovery decisions.
