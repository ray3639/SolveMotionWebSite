What It Does

SolveMotion 6.0 turns movement sessions into structured intelligence. The app captures movement context, session activity, camera evidence, training environment context, and user review signals, then renders Core 12.0 output for readiness, risk, confidence, movement quality, missing evidence, recommendation, trajectory, and session report surfaces.

Movement Quality

Interprets movement evidence into reviewable quality signals for coaching, training, performance, and education.

Live Readiness

Supports readiness visibility during and after movement sessions through Core-owned output instead of app-side scoring.

Risk and Missing Evidence

Surfaces risk and missing evidence so the system can explain uncertainty instead of overstating the quality of a movement decision.

Session Report

Environment Engine

SolveMotion 6.0 treats environment as part of movement intelligence. Weather, surface conditions, training setting, fatigue context, and session intent can change how movement recommendations should be reviewed. The Environment Engine direction allows outdoor training conditions to become part of the movement decision context.

Weather Context

Weather can help frame heat, cold, wind, rain, and outdoor readiness conditions before a movement session.

Surface Conditions

Surface conditions can influence stability, injury risk, stride quality, mobility demands, and training adaptation.

Injury Risk Review

Injury risk is treated as a review signal, not a diagnosis. It helps identify caution, uncertainty, and missing evidence.

Training Adaptation

Training adaptation guidance can explain whether the next session should be maintained, adjusted, reduced, or reviewed.

Boundaries

SolveMotion is not a medical diagnosis system and does not replace clinical evaluation, athletic training judgment, coaching expertise, or human review. Risk and injury risk language should be treated as decision-support context. Missing evidence must remain visible when the system lacks enough information to support a stronger conclusion.
