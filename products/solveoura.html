What It Does

SolveOura is being built to organize wearable and recovery context into structured intelligence surfaces. The app supports recovery readiness, sleep and strain interpretation, trend review, trajectory, adaptive guidance, missing evidence, and report-ready recovery summaries.

Recovery Readiness

Frames recovery preparedness, fatigue context, readiness direction, and review needs using Core 12.0 output.

Wearable Interpretation

Organizes wearable signals into readable recovery context without turning the app into a duplicate local scoring engine.

Sleep and Strain Context

Sleep and strain context can help explain recovery pressure, training readiness, and uncertainty in the current output.

Recovery Report

Converts recovery context into report-ready summaries, adaptive guidance, and next action review.

What We Built

SolveOura is being developed as the recovery intelligence member of the SolveMotion Labs ecosystem. The product direction includes wearable interpretation, recovery readiness, sleep and strain context, adaptive guidance, trend and trajectory, report surfaces, and render-only architecture.

Boundaries

SolveOura is not a medical diagnosis system and does not replace clinical care, emergency care, coaching judgment, athletic training judgment, sleep medicine, or professional review. Recovery readiness, sleep, strain, confidence, risk, missing evidence, adaptive guidance, and recommendations should be treated as decision-support context.

No Local Computation - No Duplicate Logic - Human-Governed Intelligence - Release Gate Discipline

Core 12.0 Alignment

SolveOura is being aligned to the SolveMotion Labs Core 12.0 architecture. The app does not own hidden scoring, duplicate recovery logic, or local recommendation engines. It collects structured recovery context, emits DomainSignal input, and renders immutable DTO output from CoreIntelligenceOrchestrator.
