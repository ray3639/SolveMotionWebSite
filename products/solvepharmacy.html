Boundaries

SolvePharmacy is an education and knowledge-support system. It is not medical advice, a prescribing system, a medication safety authority, or a replacement for pharmacists, clinicians, pharmacy policy, or professional judgment. Risk, safety, confidence, missing evidence, and recommendations should be treated as educational decision-support context.
