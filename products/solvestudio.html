Plan the practical details

Hosting, domains and ongoing responsibilities.

Guided discovery helps you describe your preferences before a proposal is agreed. Discuss who owns and manages each part of your software delivery.

HostingClient-owned or managed

Describe your existing environment or a managed-hosting preference, along with any database, API and service needs.

DomainsExisting or new

Identify domain ownership, registration and renewal responsibility. Describe scoped DNS and deployment access procedures without entering passwords or access keys.

ResponsibilitiesSupport beyond launch

Discuss backup, maintenance, support, migration and transfer responsibilities so they can be recorded in the proposed agreement.

Costs & agreementSeparate, explicit decisions

Discuss recurring domain, hosting and third-party costs. Any optional hosting or Care proposal records its own actual price, terms and responsibilities and requires separate agreement.

Planning answers are preferences. Saving or approving a project proposal does not select an optional service, start billing, authorize provisioning or establish deployment and rollback readiness.

Canonical Studio workflow

A governed path from planning to delivery

Discovery → Requirements → Blueprint → Review → Owner Approval → Client Review → Proposal → Client Approval → Commercial/Payment State → Development Authorization → Delivery.

These are distinct review and authorization stages, not automatic transitions or a promise that every project has reached them. Proposal approval and a payment record do not independently grant build, deployment or private workspace authority.

This workflow shows the project process. Each stage requires its own evidence and human decisions. Creating a draft does not approve payment, development, hosting or release.
