Player guide

How to play SolveThrone.

Build a settlement, prepare your survivors and bring your expedition home. The in-game Field manual explains each system as you explore.

Start your settlement

Follow the opening objectives. In Settlement, choose a structure and select a free tile connected to the southern road. Complete shelter, food production and a usable workshop before expanding.

Put survivors to work

Open Survivors to manage work and equipment. Keep healthy people at home for construction and gathering. Building crews pause their usual gathering while they work.

Craft and equip

Open Workshop, review a recipe's materials and place an order. When an item is ready, equip it from Survivors. Prepare tools, armor and supplies before assigning your expedition squad.

Choose your route

Assign healthy home survivors to Squad. Open World map, select an unlocked route and confirm travel. Check the food cost, weather and squad readiness before leaving.

Read the battlefield

Move to adjacent open tiles and use cover. Read the enemy's visible intent before choosing Attack, Guard or supplies. A successful field action gives the enemy its response.

Collect and return

Approach a cache or refuge before choosing Collect or Rescue. Defeat the threat and extract from the western edge to keep your rewards and cargo. Back home, check Defense for damage and repair needs.

Mouse and keyboard

Click a tile to select it. With the board focused, use the arrow keys to move the cursor and Enter to choose a tile. Press R to rotate the selected wall or gate, or use the on-screen Rotate action.

Hold the middle mouse button to pan the settlement. Hold Ctrl and scroll to zoom around the pointer. Press Escape to return to the controls.

Save and make it comfortable

Use Save settlement before leaving and the game's exit controls to finish safely. Return through the same browser and site for your local settlement.

Use Pause / resume when you need time to plan in a local game. In Settings, adjust Text size or Mute all sound. Open Journal, then Read field manual for more help.

Game progress and release readiness.

The Build 14 offline browser release supports settlement building, crafting and expeditions, connected wall and gate placement, building relocation, and a visible road into World map exploration. The wider game continues to develop.

Player accounts, multiplayer and cross-device profiles remain in development and are not publicly released. Optional paid cosmetics are planned, and real payments remain inactive. Core integration remains outside the current game scope. No store availability or release date is being announced.

Browser play is available above. Public Windows, Android and iOS downloads remain subject to their separate release processes. An Android App Bundle is a store-upload artifact.
