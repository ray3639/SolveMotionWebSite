Public-site privacy information.

Reading public pages involves ordinary connection information processed by the hosting provider. Opening an external source or download contacts that destination, whose own practices apply.

The contact action opens your email application. If you send a message, the information you include travels through your email provider and is used to respond. Send only the product or page, a general description and information you are authorized to share. Keep passwords, patient records and confidential project or investigation material out of initial inquiries.

Some links have interest-event hooks. The website can pass a short product/action event name to an analytics handler if one is present. This is separate from app record storage; it is not a promise that hosting or browser services collect no connection data.

Each application has its own data practices. The public website does not grant access to private workspaces.

Accessibility and feedback.

Public pages use semantic headings, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus and layouts designed for small screens. Accessibility work continues across browsers, text sizes and assistive technology.

If something prevents you from using a page, contact SolveMotion Labs with the page address, the action you tried and any browser or assistive-technology details you choose to share. Do not include private records. This information helps identify the barrier; it is not a claim of completed accessibility certification.
